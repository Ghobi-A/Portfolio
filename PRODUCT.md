# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers, alongside technical reviewers. The portfolio should give both groups a clear high-level account of the work, while allowing technical readers to inspect methods and evidence.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Ghobikan Aravindan's applied data science work. It presents selected projects and provides paths to explore their evidence and contact Ghobikan. Success means visitors can understand the work's value, inspect relevant technical depth, and find a clear way to make contact.

## Positioning

The work connects behavioural intelligence, evaluated machine learning, privacy and fairness audits, data engineering, and deployed data products. Project evidence and limitations are part of the presentation, not optional fine print.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive with different levels of technical familiarity and limited time. Recruiters need a quick overview; technical reviewers may follow project, methods, source, and case-study links for detail.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dependency-free static site authored in semantic HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; GitHub Pages publishes the generated `_site` output.
- Seven featured areas: behavioural demand intelligence, distributed image ML, Crestbound Duelists, restaurant ordering and operations, privacy–utility and fairness, Creative Audio Lab, and the Semantic Drift research protocol.
- Native HTML disclosures, project navigation, source links, a CV, and contact links are existing site capabilities.
- Preserve project facts and distinguish implemented work, measured results, proposed changes, and future experiments. Do not invent outcomes, metrics, customer evidence, or finished assets.
- Existing build, metadata, and deployment workflow are documented in README.md and docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md.

## Brand Commitments

The approved identity combines Study Gallery clarity with Proof Print's navy, pink and yellow palette, serif typography, authentic project imagery and research-dossier depth. GitHub icons remain compact; technical details stay available beside readable project stories.

## Evidence on Hand

Project evidence, caveats, source records, and visual-claim boundaries are recorded in docs/EVIDENCE.md. The portfolio includes measured charts, explanatory diagrams, case studies, public project links, and a CV. Do not treat conceptual diagrams as experimental results.

## Product Principles

- Make the high-level story clear to both hiring and technical audiences.
- Keep deeper methods and evidence available without forcing every visitor through them.
- Make claims traceable to real project evidence.
- State limitations and project maturity honestly.
- Keep an obvious path to contact.
